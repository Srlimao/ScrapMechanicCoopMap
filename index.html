Scrap Mechanic Tactical Tablet Guide

1. In-Game Overlays & Display Modes

Seamlessly play Scrap Mechanic with in-game tactical heads-up overlays without alt-tabbing (requires Borderless Windowed or Windowed mode):

  • All In Game Mode [M]: Press M anytime in-game to summon the full interactive tactical map over your game. Press Esc, M, or click Return to Game to dismiss the map and automatically restore game focus and 3D mouse look.
  • Radar In Game Mode [F9]: Proximity radar floats unobtrusively over the game. Press F9 in-game to unlock the radar HUD for dragging and resizing.
  • Game-Focus Protected Hotkeys: Shortcuts (M / F9) only trigger when Scrap Mechanic is the active window, preventing conflicts while typing in Discord, browser, or other apps.
  • Collapsible Sidebar & POI Waypoints: In-game mode automatically starts with a collapsed left sidebar and collapsed sub-filters for maximum screen visibility. The radar displays surrounding POIs (Mechanic, Trader, Packing, Growlabs) with live distance and perimeter waypoints.

2. Live GPS & Player Tracking

When Scrap Mechanic is running, the app automatically hooks into the game process via a secure, read-only memory scanner at 60 FPS.

  • Follow Player: Toggle camera lock to automatically follow your character's live coordinates and heading as you explore.
  • Status Badge: Top-left badge indicates live connection status ( ONLINE). Click retry icon if reconnecting after restarting the game.

3. Tactical Proximity Radar & DLL Bridge

A 360° military scope in the bottom-right corner powered by the bundled Proxy DLL Bridge (dinput8.dll) for real-time threat detection.

  • Hostile Threat Arrows: Farmbots, Tapebots, Haybots, and Totebots render as pure red icons with clear directional altitude arrows (▲ Above, ▼ Below, ● Same Level).
  • Passive & Wildlife Filtering: Seedbots, Caged Farmers, Traders, Wocs, and Glowbugs appear as soft green dots and never trigger danger alerts.
  • Creations & Vehicles: Dynamic vehicles with 50+ blocks are tracked with distance and altitude chevrons.
  • 1-Click Radar Install & Restart: If the DLL is missing, click "Install Radar" and "Restart Game" in the radar footer or browse your custom game folder under Settings (⚙️).

4. Loading Your World Save

Click Select Save in the top header or drag & drop your .db save file anywhere onto the window.

%AppData%\Axolot Games\Scrap Mechanic\User\User_<SteamID>\Save\Survival\<SaveName>.db

Loading a save parses all 12,288 world cells, Point-of-Interests, Warehouses, Trader hideouts, Oil nodes, player chests, and built vehicles.

5. Multiplayer Co-op Squad Radar

  • Host / Join Room: Click the Squad button in the header. Create a 6-character room or enter an existing code to link up with friends.
  • Teammate Beacons: Teammates stream their live coordinates and heading arrows with custom chosen beacon colors in real time.
  • World Sync: The host automatically uploads the world cell definitions to squad members with zero port forwarding required.

6. Tactical Tools & Features

  • Inspector Panel: Click any landmark, vehicle, chest, trader, or robot on the map to inspect coordinates, contents, and health in the slide-out inspector.
  • Measure Tool: Click Measure then pick any two points on the map to measure exact distance in blocks and meters.
  • Layer Filters: Toggle individual overlays in the left sidebar (Warehouses, Traders, Oil Ponds, Ruin Buildings, Cotton, Beacons, and Vehicles).
  • Bookmarks & Search: Save custom waypoints with notes or search POIs and creations instantly.
  • Snapshot: Click Snapshot to capture and download a crisp, high-resolution PNG export of your current map view.

7. Navigation & Shortcuts

  • Left Click + Drag — Pan the tactical map.
  • Mouse Wheel — Smooth zoom in and out.
  • Left Click — Inspect entities, POIs, and creations.
  • Esc — Close active modals and deactivate measurement tool.
