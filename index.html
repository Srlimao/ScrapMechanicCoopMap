Scrap Mechanic Map Viewer & Live Tracker Guide

Real-Time Player Tracking

When running Scrap Mechanic, your character's position and orientation stream live directly to this map viewer. Click Follow Player to lock the map camera to your character as you travel!

Loading Your Own Save File

Click Upload Save (.db) or drag and drop your survival save database directly onto the map. Default save path:

%AppData%\Roaming\Axolot Games\Scrap Mechanic\User\User_<SteamID>\Save\Survival\<SaveName>.db

Navigation & Controls

  • Pan: Click and drag anywhere on the canvas.
  • Zoom: Mouse wheel scroll or use the +/- buttons.
  • Follow Player: Camera stays locked and centered on your moving character.
  • Coordinates Toggle: Click the Coordinates button in the top bar to show/hide world and cell coordinate overlays.
  • Inspect: Click any entity to view in-depth details, dimensions, and teleport commands.
  • Measure: Click the Measure button and click 2 points to calculate distances in meters, blocks, and travel time.

100% Client-Side & Private

This viewer operates locally on your machine. No save data or personal files are ever transmitted to any external server.
